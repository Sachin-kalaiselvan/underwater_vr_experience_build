# Asset register

Three sources: CC0 photoscans (Poly Haven, Smithsonian), assets generated by scripts in this repo, and Unity's own packages. No marketplace models, no stock audio. CC0 = public domain, fine for commercial use, no attribution required (credited here anyway).

Status key: **first pass** = made, exported, imported cleanly, but not final art yet. **final** = signed off. Nothing is final yet.

## Photoscanned models (the main art now)

Real-world scans, decimated for Quest by `Tools/blender/import_scans.py` (welds, strips the scanned ground skirt off rocks, decimates, bakes a weathering / living-colour tint into vertex colour, copies the scan's albedo + normal). Rendered with `ABYSS/UnderwaterTextured`.

The Smithsonian corals are museum skeletons, so they're bleached white in the scan. The living colour comes from the vertex tint; the surface detail is the real scan.

## Audio

All synthesised from scratch by `Tools/audio/make_audio.py` (numpy, no samples). 44.1 kHz mono wav, Unity compresses to Vorbis on import.

| Clip | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|
| amb_underwater_loop | 30 s loop | ambience |
| amb_bubbles_loop | 20 s loop | ambience |
| ui_hover, ui_click, ui_menu_open | <0.4 s | menus |
| int_select, int_fail, int_discover | <1 s | interaction feedback |
| int_step_complete, int_activity_complete | <2 s | activities |
| nav_teleport | 0.5 s | fades |

Status: first pass. Functional and gentle, but a sound designer should replace the ambience before a client demo.
