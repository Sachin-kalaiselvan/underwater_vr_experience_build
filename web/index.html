About

ABYSS

An interactive underwater exhibition with five connected areas: the Descent, the Reef, an Exhibition clearing, a Shipwreck and the Deep. Built as a Meta Quest app in Unity; this is the browser version of the same world, with the same assets.

The corals are photoscans of real specimens from the Smithsonian's coral collection. Rocks, props and the ship are photoscans from Poly Haven. All CC0.
